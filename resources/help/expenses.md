Claim money you have spent for work: travel, subsistence, taxis, parking, materials, or mileage.

## Claiming

1. **Finance › Expenses**, fill in the claim: date, event (if it was for one), category, description and amount.
2. **Attach a receipt**: it is required, except for mileage.
3. **Mileage**: enter the miles; Hive pays 45p a mile (set in [Settings](/help/settings)).
4. Foreign amounts use finance’s latest exchange rate on the date of the claim.

## Approval

The event’s producer (or your line manager, for non-event costs) approves first, then finance. A rejection always comes with a reason. Approved expenses become actual cost on the event, on the line for their category, and appear in the expenses export for payment.
