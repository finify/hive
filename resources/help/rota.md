The **rota** shows who is doing what, over two weeks or a month: one row per person, with bars for bookings, holds, leave and unavailability.

## Reading it

- **Solid** bars are confirmed bookings; lighter bars are first and second holds.
- **Leave** shows as “Leave”, or **Absent** for sensitive types such as sickness, unless you are HR or the person’s line manager.
- **Unavailable** is a freelancer’s own blocked dates.
- A **Clash** marker means someone is booked twice on the same day. See [Clashes](/help/clashes).

## Using it

- Switch between **2 weeks** and **Month**, move with the arrows, or jump back to today.
- Filter by department, employment type or skill, or show only people with bookings.
- Select a booking to open the event.

Producers and heads of department see everyone; others see the rota for their own work.
