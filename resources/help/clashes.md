A **clash** is when someone is booked on two things on the same day: two events, or an event and approved leave or blocked dates.

## How clashes happen

Hive warns whenever a booking overlaps another and asks for a reason before it saves. A clash can still happen when, for example, leave is approved after a booking was made. The producers of both events are told.

## Resolving a clash

Open **Resourcing › Clashes**, or the **Clash** marker on the rota or crew tab, and choose **Resolve**:

- **Move the days to someone else**: pick which booking changes, and Hive suggests people free for those dates with the same role and skill, nearest and best match first. The original person is released from those days and told.
- **Keep both bookings**: record why both can stand (for example, a short morning call and an evening show nearby). The clash stays visible but no longer counts as open.

Only the producers of the events involved, or the operations director, can resolve a clash. To drop someone entirely, use **Release** on the event’s Crew tab. Everything you choose is logged. Ask Hive can list clashes: “Who is double-booked this week?”
