**Admin › Users and roles** controls who can sign in and what they can do.

## Inviting someone

Enter their email and choose a role, then **Send invitation**. They get an email to set a password; the link lasts seven days. Waiting invitations are listed on the page.

To change what someone can do, choose **Roles** next to them and tick the roles they need.

## Roles

| Role | In short |
|---|---|
| Super admin | Everything, including settings and users |
| Operations director | All events, budgets and approvals above producer limits; reports |
| Head of department | Crew and rota for their department, approves their team’s time and leave |
| Producer | Runs events: crew, budgets, POs, approvals for their events |
| Finance | Budgets, invoices, rates, exports, payroll locks |
| HR | People, leave, documents, restricted details, onboarding, data protection |
| Employee | Own work, time, leave and expenses |
| Freelancer | Own bookings, time, invoices and expenses |
| Client viewer | Read-only view of their own events |

Some roles must use [two-factor](/help/security).

## Deactivating

**Deactivate** an account when someone leaves. They can no longer sign in, and their history stays. **Reactivate** brings it back. To remove personal details later, see [Data protection](/help/data-protection).
