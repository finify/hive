Each event has a **budget**: lines by category (venue, AV, crew, travel, accommodation and so on), each with a budgeted amount. Hive tracks every cost against those lines.

## The figures

| Figure | Means |
|---|---|
| **Budget** | The approved budget lines added up |
| **Committed** | Money promised but not yet invoiced: approved purchase orders |
| **Actual** | Money spent: approved invoices, approved freelancer time, expenses, travel |
| **Remaining estimate** | What is still expected on each line, beyond committed and actual |
| **Forecast** | Actual + committed + remaining estimate: what the event will cost |
| **Margin** | Revenue less forecast, in pounds and as a percentage |
| **Health** | On track, Watch or At risk (see [Dashboard](/help/dashboard)) |

Foreign currency costs keep their original amount and the rate used, and are shown in pounds.

## Changing a budget

On the event’s **Budget** tab, people who can edit budgets add or change lines. The first approved version is the **baseline**. Any change after that starts a **revision**, which needs the operations director’s approval; until then you see how far it is above or below the baseline.

**Raise PO** next to a line starts a purchase order for it. **Explain budget risk** asks [Ask Hive](/help/ask-hive) to explain the forecast.

## Revenue

Client fee, change requests and other revenue lines are on the Budget tab. Billing milestones are in [Client billing](/help/billing).

## The Budgets page

**Finance › Budgets** lists every event whose budget you can see, with forecast, margin and health.
