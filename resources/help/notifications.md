## The bell

The bell (top right) shows your notices: booking requests, releases, clashes, things to approve, decisions on your leave and expenses, documents expiring, event stage changes and more. The number is how many are unread. **Mark all as read** clears them.

## Email settings

Go to **Notifications** › **Email settings** (or your name › **Notification settings**). Notices always appear under the bell; you choose which also come by email:

- Bookings, releases and travel
- Clashes on your events
- Things waiting for your approval
- Timesheets returned or approved
- Leave decisions and TOIL
- Documents expiring
- Purchase orders
- Invoices and disputes
- Client billing
- Budget health warnings
- Onboarding and offboarding
- Event stage changes

### Daily digest

Choose **A daily digest** to get one email at 08:00 with everything from the day before, instead of one email per notice. Only the types you ticked are included, and you get nothing if nothing happened.

## Your calendar feed

Hive can put your bookings, holds, leave and travel into Outlook, Google or Apple Calendar.

1. On the same page, choose **Turn on my calendar feed**.
2. **Copy the address straight away**: for security it is only shown once.
3. In your calendar app, add a calendar “from URL” or “by subscription” and paste it.

It updates about every hour. Holds and leave waiting for approval show as tentative. An event’s own calendar (its phases) is at the same address with `/events/EV-26117.ics` in place of `.ics`.

Anyone with the address can read it. If it might have been shared, choose **Get a new address**: the old one stops working at once. **Turn off** removes it.
