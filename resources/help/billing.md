## Client billing

Each event’s fee is billed in **milestones**: for example 50% on confirmation and 50% after the event. Client invoices are raised in the accounting system; Hive tracks them.

- Add milestones by amount or as a share of the event’s revenue.
- **Mark invoiced** with the accounting system’s reference.
- **Mark paid** when the money arrives.
- Upcoming and overdue milestones show on finance’s dashboard.

## Exports

**Finance › Exports** downloads CSV files for a date range:

- **Approved supplier invoices**, for payment runs.
- **Approved freelancer time**, with day rates (needs rate access), for paying freelancers.
- **Approved expenses**, for reimbursement.
- **Billing**, milestones and their status.

Each export is logged. Files open cleanly in Excel.
