## Asking for leave

**People › Leave › Ask for leave**: choose the type and dates, and add a note if you like. Hive counts **working days only**: weekends and your region’s bank holidays don’t count. Your line manager (or HR) decides, and you are told. You can cancel leave that hasn’t started.

The approver sees any bookings your leave would overlap before deciding. Approved leave shows on the rota and stops you being booked.

## Your balance

The **Annual leave left** figure shows your entitlement for the leave year (allowance plus days carried over), less leave **taken** and **booked**. Requests waiting for approval also count against what you can still ask for.

- The leave year runs from 1 January.
- Up to **5 unused days** carry into the next year when it opens.

Ask Hive can tell you too: “How much leave do I have left?”

## Time off in lieu (TOIL)

Employees earn TOIL when **approved** event time falls on a **weekend or bank holiday**: up to one day for each day worked (hours over 7.5, to the nearest half day). Take it by asking for leave of type **Time off in lieu**; you can’t take more than you have earned.

## Leave types

Annual leave, Sick, Compassionate, Parental, Unpaid, Time off in lieu and Training. **Sick, compassionate and parental leave are private**: only HR, the line manager and the person see the type; everyone else sees **Absent**.

## For HR and line managers

- **Record an absence** for sickness or anything not asked for in advance. It is recorded as approved. Line managers can do this for their own reports.
- **Balances** (HR and directors): everyone’s allowance, taken, booked, waiting and TOIL. HR can **Change** a person’s allowance and carry-over.
- **Bank holidays**: England and Wales, Scotland and Northern Ireland. HR can add or remove days.
