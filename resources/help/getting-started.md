Hive is where Lumen & Co Live runs its work: events, the people who deliver them, their time, the money, and HR. Everything you need for a job is in one place, and you only see what your role allows.

## How it’s laid out

- **The sidebar** lists the areas your role can use, grouped as Home, Events, Resourcing, Time, Finance, People, Directory, Reports and Admin. Hover over any item to see what it is for.
- **The “?” next to each page title** opens the guide for that page. All the guides are listed under **Help** in the sidebar.
- **Search** (top of every page, or press **/**) finds events, people, clients and purchase orders by name or code.
- **The bell** shows your notices. **Ask Hive** answers questions in plain English.

## Rules Hive follows everywhere

- **You only see what your role allows.** Rates, budgets and restricted personal details are hidden unless you have access. If something is missing, it is usually your role, not a fault.
- **Nobody approves their own work.** Timesheets, expenses, leave and spend always go to someone else.
- **Everything is logged.** Every change records who made it and when, in the audit log and on the record’s Activity tab.
- **Money adds up.** Totals are always the sum of the lines shown, in pounds and pence.
- **Dates** are shown as 23 Oct 2026 and times in 24-hour UK time.

## Your first day

1. Sign in with the invitation you were sent (or **Sign in with Microsoft**, if your organisation has turned it on). Some roles must set up [two-factor](/help/security) first.
2. Look at your [Dashboard](/help/dashboard): its **Needs attention** list is your to-do list.
3. Check [My work](/help/my-work) for bookings and anything waiting for you.
4. Set which notices you want by email in [Notifications and calendar](/help/notifications), and turn on your calendar feed.

## Getting help

If something looks wrong, check the record’s **Activity** tab first: it shows every change. For access problems, ask HR or a Hive administrator.
