**Admin › Settings** holds the limits and thresholds Hive works to. Anyone with settings access can read them; only an administrator can change them. Changes apply at once and are logged with the old and new values.

| Group | Settings |
|---|---|
| Approvals and spending | Producer and operations director PO limits, when approvers are reminded, mileage rate |
| Budget health and invoices | Forecast share of budget that moves an event to Watch, target margin, invoice tolerance (percentage and amount) |
| Working time | Minimum rest, long shift, when a break is needed and how long, weekly average |
| People | Whether an expired mandatory document blocks or only warns, default annual leave, most days carried over |
| Ask Hive | On or off, questions per person per hour, how hard Claude thinks |
| Data retention | How long timesheets, expenses, leavers’ records, expired documents and the Ask Hive log are kept |

Passwords and keys for Microsoft, Xero and Claude are not here: they are set on the server. See [Integrations](/help/integrations).
