**Approvals** is one inbox for everything waiting for your decision, oldest first: timesheet entries, leave, purchase orders, supplier invoices, expenses and freelancer invoices.

## Deciding

- **Approve** moves an item on. Some items need more than one approval (a large PO, or an expense that goes to the producer then finance); Hive sends it to the next person.
- **Return** or **Reject** needs a comment saying why. The person is told.
- Working time **flags** on timesheet entries show what to check first. **Summarise timesheets** asks [Ask Hive](/help/ask-hive) for a summary.

You never see your own submissions: **nobody approves their own work**.

## Who approves what

| Item | Approved by |
|---|---|
| Timesheet entry | The producer of that event (internal time: line manager) |
| Leave | The line manager, or HR |
| Purchase order | The producer up to their limit; above it, the operations director |
| Supplier invoice | The producer of the event; over tolerance, finance too |
| Expense | The producer (or line manager), then finance |
| Freelancer invoice | Finance |

PO limits and invoice tolerance are in [Settings](/help/settings).

## Cover while you’re away

Under **Cover while you’re away**, choose **Who approves for you**, the dates, and **Set cover**. Your items appear in their inbox for those dates, and they see a note saying they are covering. **End** stops it early.

Approvers with items waiting more than three days get a reminder on Monday mornings.
