**My work** is everything that is about you: what you are booked on, what you need to answer, and your paperwork.

## Booking requests

When a producer wants to book a freelancer, a request appears here with the event, dates, role, call time and location. **Accept booking** or **Decline** it. A producer sees your answer straight away. Employees are booked directly and don’t get requests.

## Upcoming bookings

Your confirmed bookings and holds, with dates, location, role and call time.

- **Confirmed**: you are working.
- **First hold**: you are pencilled in and should keep the dates.
- **Second hold**: you are a back-up if the first hold falls through.

Released or declined bookings are listed underneath.

## My travel

Flights, trains, transfers and hotels booked for you, with references and notes. You are told by notice and email when travel is added, changed or cancelled.

## My documents

Insurance, certificates and contracts on file, with expiry dates. If a **mandatory** document (insurance, right to work) has expired, producers cannot confirm you, so upload the renewal before it runs out. You get reminders 30 and 7 days before.

## Dates I can’t work

Freelancers can mark dates as unavailable. They show on the rota, and producers see a warning if they try to book you.

## Clashes

If you are double-booked, it shows here. Your producers have been told and will resolve it.
