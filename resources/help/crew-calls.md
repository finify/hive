A **crew call** is a role an event needs: for example “AV Technician × 2, 20–21 Oct, call 07:00, wrap 19:00”. People are booked onto crew calls.

## Adding a crew call

On the event › **Crew and resourcing** › **Add crew call**: the role, skill and minimum level (1 to 5), how many people, dates, phase, call and wrap times, location, budgeted day rate and notes.

## Booking someone

1. Choose **Book** on the crew call. Hive suggests people free for those dates, best match first. **Suggest crew** asks [Ask Hive](/help/ask-hive) for the same.
2. Choose the person and the status: **Second hold**, **First hold** or **Confirmed**.
3. Hive checks before saving:
   - **Clashes**: if they are already booked, you must give a reason to book them anyway (see [Clashes](/help/clashes)).
   - **Working time**: a warning if they would have under 11 hours’ rest, a shift over 12 hours, or more than 48 hours a week on average.
   - **Documents**: someone with an expired mandatory document (insurance, right to work) can be held but **not confirmed** until it is renewed.
4. **Freelancers** get a booking request they accept or decline on [My work](/help/my-work). **Employees** are booked directly.

**Confirm first holds** confirms every first hold on the event in one go, skipping any that can’t be confirmed. **Release** frees someone and tells them.

## Call sheets

Pick a day under **Call sheet for** and choose **PDF** to see that day’s call sheet: crew, roles, call times, venue, contacts. **Email to crew** sends it to everyone booked that day.

## The Crew calls page

**Resourcing › Crew calls** lists open crew calls across your events, so you can see where places still need filling.
