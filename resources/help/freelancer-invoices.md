Freelancers are paid for **approved** time. Each invoice covers a period.

## For freelancers

- **Upload** your invoice for a period. Hive shows what it expected from your approved time at your day rate; a difference needs a note.
- If you have a **self-billing agreement** on file, Hive can raise the invoice for you from your approved time. You don’t need to send one.

## For finance

Invoices go to finance for approval. Approving an invoice doesn’t add cost to the event: the cost was posted when the time was approved, so nothing is counted twice. Approved invoices appear in the freelancer payment export (see [Client billing and exports](/help/billing)).
