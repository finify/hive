Everyone who works on events records their time in a **weekly timesheet**, Monday to Sunday.

## Recording time

1. **Time › My timesheets**, pick the week.
2. Add an entry: the date, what you worked on (an event, or an internal code such as office or training), start and end times, and your break. Freelancers paid by the day can enter days.
3. Add a note if anything was unusual.

Hive flags entries that break working time rules (a long shift, no break, short rest, more hours than the crew call, time on a day you weren’t booked). Flags don’t stop you; they tell the approver what to check.

## Submitting

Choose **Submit** at the end of the week. Each entry goes to the producer of its event (internal time to your line manager). You can change entries until they are approved.

## If an entry is returned

You get a notice with the approver’s comment. Fix the entry and submit again; only that entry goes back for approval.

## After approval

- **Freelancers**: approved event time becomes cost on the event at your day rate, and feeds your invoice (see [Freelancer invoices](/help/freelancer-invoices)).
- **Employees**: approved event time on a weekend or bank holiday earns **time off in lieu** (see [Leave](/help/leave)).

Once finance has locked a period for payroll, its time can’t be changed. There is a reminder on Fridays if your week isn’t submitted.
