The **Travel** tab on an event lists everyone’s journeys and hotels, grouped by person. Hive records travel booked elsewhere (by you or the travel agent); it doesn’t book with airlines or hotels.

## Adding travel

1. Open the event › **Travel** › **Add travel** (or **Add journey** / **Add hotel** next to a person).
2. Choose who and what: **Outbound**, **Return**, **Ground transport** or **Accommodation**.
3. For a journey: how (flight, rail, car, coach, ferry, taxi), from, to, departure and arrival (**local time** where it departs, as on the ticket), operator and reference.
4. For a hotel: the hotel, check-in and check-out.
5. Enter the cost and currency.

Only people booked on the event, its team or its producer can have travel on it. The traveller is told and sees it on [My work](/help/my-work) and in their calendar feed.

## How costs reach the budget

The cost goes straight onto the event’s **Travel** line (or **Accommodation** for hotels) as an actual cost. If you change the cost, only the difference is posted; if you cancel, the cost is taken off. Nothing is ever edited in place, so the budget history always adds up.

## The manifest

**Manifest (CSV)** downloads everyone’s travel for the travel agent or the client. **Passport details and access needs are only included for people allowed to see restricted details** (normally HR), and every download is logged. Costs are only included for people who can see the budget.
