The **skills catalogue** lists every skill Hive tracks, grouped by category (technical, production, creative, registration, languages…), and who has each one.

- Levels run from **1** (basic) to **5** (expert).
- A **tick** means someone has verified the level.
- Search by skill or by a person’s name.

Skills are what [Availability](/help/availability) and crew suggestions use to rank people. Levels are set on a person’s profile by HR.
