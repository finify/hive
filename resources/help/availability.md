**Availability** finds people who are free for a role and dates.

1. Choose the role, the dates and, if needed, a skill and minimum level.
2. Hive lists people who are **not** booked, on leave or unavailable on any of those dates, best match first (skill level, then rating).
3. Book from the event’s Crew tab, or open a person to see their profile.

Day rates show only if your role can see rates. Freelancers with an expired mandatory document are flagged: you can hold them but not confirm them until it is renewed.

Ask Hive can do the same: “Who’s free for the Lisbon build?”
