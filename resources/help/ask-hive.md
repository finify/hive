**Ask Hive** answers questions about Hive’s own records in plain English, and can set up everyday changes for you to confirm. Open it with the **Ask Hive** button at the top of any page.

## What you can ask

- “Which events are at risk this month and why?”
- “Who’s free for the Lisbon build?”
- “Who is double-booked this week?”
- “What’s waiting for my approval?”
- “Who is off next week?”
- “How much leave do I have left?”
- “Tell me about EV-26114.”
- “What’s left to do on EV-26114?” or “Who’s on the crew?”
- “Find me a lighting designer in Manchester.”
- “Which supplier invoices are disputed?” or “Run the utilisation report for October.”

## Asking how Hive works

Ask Hive knows this staff guide. Ask it what a page, tab, status or term means, or how to do something, and it answers in a few steps with a link to the guide it used:

- “How do I submit my timesheet?”
- “What does first hold mean?”
- “Where do I find the call sheet?”
- “How do I resolve a clash?”

It only uses the guides your role can see on the Help page. If it can make the change for you, it offers to.

It remembers the conversation, so you can follow up: “and the week after?”, “what about Kofi?”. The conversation stays with you as you move between pages until you sign out or your session ends; **Start again** clears it sooner.

Answers come with **links** to the records they used (events, people, pages), and a line saying which records Ask Hive looked at.

## Asking it to do something

You can ask Ask Hive to make everyday changes for you:

| You can say | Who can |
|---|---|
| “Book annual leave from 16 to 18 Nov”, “Cancel my leave on 3 Nov” | Anyone with leave |
| “Add 9 to 5.30 on EV-26114 yesterday, half an hour break”, “Submit this week’s timesheet” | Anyone with a timesheet |
| “Accept the Lisbon booking”, “I can’t work 1 to 2 Dec” | Freelancers |
| “Claim 42 miles to the Riverside site visit” | Anyone with expenses |
| “Approve Kofi’s entries for Tuesday”, “Send Monday back, the break is missing” | Approvers |
| “Approve Freya’s leave” | Leave approvers |
| “Add a task to confirm lectern mics, for Tomasz, due Friday”, “Mark the venue visit done” | Event team |
| “Put Ade on first hold for the AV call”, “Release Mei Lin from EV-26114” | Producers |
| “Tick ‘Laptop returned’ for Liam” | Checklist owners |

Ask Hive never makes the change itself. It shows you a **proposed change** with what will happen, and nothing happens until you press **Confirm**. Press **Cancel** if it’s wrong, or tell it what to change. Hive checks the change first, exactly as the page would: if your role can’t do it, or something is wrong (dates the wrong way round, a week already locked), Ask Hive tells you why instead.

A confirmed change is the same as making it on the page: the right people are told, and it appears in the audit log with your name. A proposal can be confirmed for 30 minutes.

Payments, budgets, purchase orders, event stages and deleting records stay on their pages; Ask Hive will tell you where.

## Buttons that ask for you

Some pages have an Ask Hive button that drafts something:

| Button | Where | What you get |
|---|---|---|
| Summarise timesheets | Approvals, Timesheets tab | Entries waiting for you, flagged ones first |
| Explain budget risk | Event, Budget tab | What is driving the forecast and what to check |
| Draft wrap report | Event page, from Live onwards | A draft to edit: delivery, crew, open items, money |
| Suggest crew | Event, Crew tab | Up to three free people for each open place |

Use **Copy** under an answer to paste a draft into an email or document.

## What it won’t do

- **It never changes anything on its own.** Every change waits for your Confirm, and it can only do what your role can do on the page.
- **It only sees what you can see.** If your role can’t see budgets, it says so rather than guessing. Rates, sickness details and passport numbers are never sent to it.
- **It doesn’t guess.** If Hive has no record that answers the question, it says so.

## Good to know

- Answers can take a few seconds; the panel fills in when ready.
- There is an hourly limit per person (set by an administrator).
- Questions, answers and confirmed changes are logged, like every other action in Hive.
- Always check a draft before you send it on.
