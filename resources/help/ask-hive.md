**Ask Hive** answers questions about Hive’s own records in plain English. Open it with the **Ask Hive** button at the top of any page.

## What you can ask

- “Which events are at risk this month and why?”
- “Who’s free for the Lisbon build?”
- “Who is double-booked this week?”
- “What’s waiting for my approval?”
- “Who is off next week?”
- “How much leave do I have left?”
- “Tell me about EV-26114.”

Answers come with **links** to the records they used (events, people, pages), and a line saying which records Ask Hive looked at.

## Buttons that ask for you

Some pages have an Ask Hive button that drafts something:

| Button | Where | What you get |
|---|---|---|
| Summarise timesheets | Approvals, Timesheets tab | Entries waiting for you, flagged ones first |
| Explain budget risk | Event, Budget tab | What is driving the forecast and what to check |
| Draft wrap report | Event page, from Live onwards | A draft to edit: delivery, crew, open items, money |
| Suggest crew | Event, Crew tab | Up to three free people for each open place |

Use **Copy** under an answer to paste a draft into an email or document.

## What it won’t do

- **It only reads.** It never approves, books, pays or deletes anything; it tells you where to do it yourself.
- **It only sees what you can see.** If your role can’t see budgets, it says so rather than guessing. Rates, sickness details and passport numbers are never sent to it.
- **It doesn’t guess.** If Hive has no record that answers the question, it says so.

## Good to know

- Answers can take a few seconds; the panel fills in when ready.
- There is an hourly limit per person (set by an administrator).
- Questions and answers are logged, like every other action in Hive.
- Always check a draft before you send it on.
