Hive keeps the documents that let people work: insurance, right to work records, certificates (IPAF, PASMA, first aid), contracts, NDAs, IR35 determinations and self-billing agreements.

## Expiry

- Each document can have an expiry date.
- The person and HR get reminders **30 and 7 days** before it expires.
- **People › Documents** lists everything expired or expiring within 30 days.

## Why it matters

Public liability insurance and right to work records are **mandatory**. Someone with an expired mandatory document can be held on a booking but **cannot be confirmed** until it is renewed (an administrator can change this to a warning in [Settings](/help/settings)).

## Uploading

On a person’s profile › **Documents**: choose the type, title, dates and file. Files are private: they open through a link that expires after a few minutes. HR sees everyone’s documents; producers and finance see whether they are valid, not the files.
