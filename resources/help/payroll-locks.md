Finance locks a period once payroll has used its time, so approved time can’t change afterwards.

## Locking

**Time › Payroll locks**: choose the period (**From** and **To**) › **Lock period**. Fully approved weeks in it become **Locked**. Nobody can add, change or remove time in a locked period.

## Unlocking

If something must be corrected, choose **Unlock** next to the lock and say why. The unlock is logged with who, when and why. Lock again once the correction is approved.

Only finance can lock and unlock.
