## Signing in

Sign in with your work email and password. There is no public sign-up: accounts come from an invitation sent by HR or an administrator. Invitations last seven days.

If your organisation has connected **Microsoft sign-in**, you can use **Sign in with Microsoft** instead. You still need a Hive account; Microsoft only confirms who you are.

## Two-factor authentication

Super admins, the operations director, finance and HR **must** use two-factor authentication. Until it is set up, Hive only shows the Security page. Anyone else can turn it on too.

1. Go to your name (top right) › **Security and two-factor**.
2. Confirm your password and choose **Turn on**.
3. Scan the QR code with an authenticator app (Microsoft Authenticator, Google Authenticator, 1Password…).
4. Enter the six-digit code to confirm.
5. **Save your recovery codes** somewhere safe. Each one signs you in once if you lose your phone.

When you sign in with Microsoft, Microsoft’s own multi-factor rules apply instead.

## Forgotten password

Use **Forgot your password?** on the sign-in page. The link is emailed to you and works once.

## If your account is deactivated

Leavers’ accounts are deactivated rather than deleted, so their history stays. If you see “This account has been deactivated”, contact HR.
