The **directory** has a record for everyone who works for the agency: employees, fixed-term staff, freelancers and agency temps.

## Finding people

**People › Directory**: search by name, job title or email, or filter by department, employment type, skill or location.

## A person’s profile

- **Overview**: job, department, line manager, location, contact.
- **Bookings**, **Timesheets**, **Leave**: their work and time.
- **Documents**: insurance, certificates and contracts (see [Documents](/help/documents)).
- **Skills**: skills and levels (see [Skills](/help/skills)).
- **Rates**: day rates by role, each from a date. Only finance, the operations director, HR and the person see them.
- **Restricted**: emergency contact, passport, dietary and access needs. Only HR and the person themselves see these; they are encrypted.

## Adding and editing

HR adds people (**New person**) and edits records. Set the person’s **bank holiday region** if they don’t work in England and Wales. Adding a leaving date lets you start their [offboarding](/help/onboarding). HR or finance add new day rates from a date; earlier rates are kept.

Accounts to sign in to Hive are separate: see [Users and roles](/help/users).

## Data protection

HR can export everything Hive holds about a person, or anonymise a leaver, from **Data protection** on their profile. See [Data protection](/help/data-protection).
