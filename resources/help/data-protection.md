Hive follows UK GDPR: we keep what we need, for as long as we need it, and people can see what we hold.

## Subject access requests

When someone asks what we hold about them, HR opens their profile › **Data protection** › **Export everything Hive holds**. The ZIP contains their records as a data file (profile, restricted details, bookings, leave, timesheets, expenses, invoices, travel, notices, Ask Hive questions, activity) and their uploaded documents. Every export is logged.

## Erasure (anonymising)

For someone who has **left** and has no bookings from today on: profile › **Data protection** › **Anonymise**, with the reason (for example the request reference).

- Their name becomes “Former staff member 21”; contact details, restricted details, documents, skills and notices are removed for good; their sign-in is closed.
- **Timesheets, costs, invoices and expenses stay**, so the accounts still add up.
- The audit log is kept as the legal record of what was done.

## Retention review

On the first of each month Hive flags records older than their retention period: timesheets and expenses (6 years), leavers’ records (6 years after leaving), expired documents (2 years) and the Ask Hive log (12 months). Periods are in [Settings](/help/settings).

**Admin › Data retention** lists them. For each one choose **Keep** or **Delete** (for leavers, **Anonymise**). Nothing is deleted until someone decides.
