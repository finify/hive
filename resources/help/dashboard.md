The dashboard is the first page you see. It shows the figures that matter for **your role**, and a **Needs attention** list.

## Needs attention

A ranked list of what needs you, most urgent first: clashes on your events, entries waiting for approval, booking requests to answer, events going over budget, documents expiring. Each line has a button that takes you straight to it. When it says “You’re clear”, nothing is waiting.

## Figures by role

| Role | You see |
|---|---|
| Operations director | Pipeline value, forecast margin across active events, clashes, events at risk or on watch, confirmed revenue by month, utilisation by department |
| Finance | Committed and actual cost this month, invoices and POs waiting, events at risk, upcoming client payments |
| HR | Starters and leavers in the next six weeks, documents expiring, leave to decide, onboarding progress |
| Producer | Your events in progress, clashes on your events, entries to approve, open crew calls, budget health of your events |
| Employee and freelancer | Your next booking, booking requests, timesheets in draft or returned, leave left (employees) |

Every figure links to the page behind it.

## Budget health

- **On track**: forecast is within 90% of the budget and margin is at or above target.
- **Watch**: forecast is over 90% of budget, or margin is below target.
- **At risk**: forecast is over budget.

Thresholds are set in [Settings](/help/settings). Each morning Hive tells the producer and operations director if an event’s health has slipped.
