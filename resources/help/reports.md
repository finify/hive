**Reports** lists the reports your role can run. Each one has filters, shows a table with a total row, and downloads as **CSV** with the same figures (money in pounds, ready for a spreadsheet). Every download is logged.

| Report | Shows | Who |
|---|---|---|
| **Event profitability** | Revenue, budget, forecast, actual and margin per event | Budget access |
| **Utilisation** | Weekdays booked as a share of weekdays, by person or department | Rota for everyone |
| **Freelancer spend** | Approved freelance time as cost, by person, role or event | Rate access |
| **Supplier spend and ratings** | POs and invoices per supplier, with rating and disputes | PO and invoice access |
| **Pipeline and win rate** | Events and fees by stage, and the share of decided pitches won | All events (fees need budget access) |
| **Leave taken and remaining** | Entitlement, taken, booked, waiting, remaining and TOIL per employee | Leave for everyone (sickness days: HR only) |
| **Timesheet compliance** | Weeks submitted on time (by noon the next Monday) and approved on time | All timesheets |
| **Working time flags** | Entries that broke a working time rule | All timesheets |

Each report only includes what your role may see: a producer’s profitability report lists events whose budget they can see, for example.
