The **calendar** shows events by month, with each phase (travel, build, rehearsal, live, breakdown) in its own shade, so you can see busy weeks at a glance.

- Switch between **Month** and **Week**, move with the arrows, or go back to **Today**.
- Select an event to open it.
- You see the events your role can see.

To have events in your own calendar app, turn on your [calendar feed](/help/notifications).
