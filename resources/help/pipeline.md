The **pipeline board** shows events as cards in columns by stage, from **Enquiry** to **Confirmed** and beyond, so you can see what is coming in and what it is worth.

- Each card shows the event code, name, client and fee.
- Each column shows its total value; the header shows this year’s win rate.
- **Drag a card** to another column to move the event; the same stage rules apply as on the event page. Or open the card and use **Move to…**.

The **Pipeline and win rate** report in [Reports](/help/reports) gives the same figures over a period, with the share of decided pitches won.
