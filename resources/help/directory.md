The **Directory** holds the organisations we work with.

## Clients

Name, sector, billing address, VAT number, payment terms, account lead and contacts. A client’s events are listed on its page.

## Suppliers

Name, category, VAT number, payment terms, whether they are **approved**, a **rating** out of 5, insurance expiry and contacts. Supplier spend and ratings are in [Reports](/help/reports).

## Venues

Address, capacity, and notes on **access** (loading bays, lifts) and **power**, so the crew arrives prepared.

Producers, finance and the operations director can add and edit; everyone with directory access can view. Many at once can be added with [Import from CSV](/help/integrations).
