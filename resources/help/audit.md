The **audit log** records every change in Hive: who did it, when, from where, and what changed (old and new values).

- Filter by area (events, resourcing, time, finance, people, identity…), by person, and by date.
- Each entry shows the record it changed and, where it applies, the old and new values.

Sign-ins and failed sign-ins are recorded too. Restricted details (passport numbers, emergency contacts) never appear in the log.

Each event, person and purchase order also has an **Activity** tab with its own history.
