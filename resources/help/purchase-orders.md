A **purchase order (PO)** is how we commit to spend with a supplier. Approved POs show as **committed** on the event’s budget.

## Raising a PO

1. **Finance › Purchase orders › New**, or **Raise PO** on a budget line.
2. Choose the event, budget line, supplier and currency, and add the lines (description, quantity, unit price, VAT).
3. **Raise as draft**, then **Submit for approval** when ready.

## Approval limits

- Up to the **producer limit**: an approving producer (not the person who raised it).
- Above it, up to the **operations director limit**: the operations director.
- Above that: the managing director too.

The limits are in [Settings](/help/settings). You are told when it is approved or returned.

## After approval

- **PDF** downloads it. **Email to supplier** sends it to the supplier’s main contact; **Mark as sent** if you sent it yourself.
- **Goods received** records delivery.
- Invoices are matched to the PO as they arrive (see [Supplier invoices](/help/supplier-invoices)).
- **Cancel PO** releases whatever hasn’t been invoiced back to the budget, with a reason.

Approvers see **Approve** and **Send back** (with a comment) on the PO.
