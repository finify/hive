# Product: Hive

Hive is the internal operations platform for Lumen & Co Live, a fictional brand experience and events agency in London and Manchester. It connects people, resourcing, time, finance and HR in one place. This is a portfolio project: every company, person and figure is fictional.

## Who uses it, and how

| Person | How often | Device | The one question they open Hive to answer |
|---|---|---|---|
| Producer | All day, in long sessions | Laptop, sometimes on site | Is my event staffed, on budget and free of clashes? |
| Operations director | A few glances a day | Laptop | Which events need me today, and what is our margin? |
| Finance | Long sessions around month end | Desktop | What is committed, what is actual, and what can I approve? |
| HR | Daily | Desktop | Whose documents, leave or onboarding need action? |
| Head of department | Daily | Laptop | Is my team booked well, and is anyone overbooked? |
| Employee | Weekly | Phone and laptop | Where am I booked, and is my time in? |
| Freelancer | Weekly, often on site | Phone | What am I booked on, and has my time been approved? |

## Surface mode

Operate. Hive is worked in, not read or marketed. Scanability, consistency and familiar patterns come before expression. The sign-in page is the one place with a little more presence, because it introduces the demo personas.

## Voice

Plain British English, sentence case, words of the trade: crew call, first hold, wrap, call sheet, committed, actual, forecast at completion. Buttons say what they do ("Approve 4 entries", "Move Tue 23 Oct to Arjun"). Errors say what happened and how to fix it. No exclamation marks, no emoji.

## Principles

1. **Answer the question first.** Every screen opens with a sentence that answers its question ("1 clash this week, on Fri 23 Oct"), then the evidence.
2. **People decide; Hive warns.** Clashes, long shifts and over-tolerance invoices are flagged, never silently blocked or auto-fixed, except where the business rules say block.
3. **Show only what you may see.** Rates, restricted fields and sensitive leave types are removed before they reach the page, not hidden with CSS.
4. **Money is exact.** Integer minor units everywhere; totals are sums of the lines shown.

## Anti-references

Generic SaaS dashboards: KPI tiles with icons in tinted circles, sparklines on every number, gradient area charts, zebra tables, colourful navigation pills, "Welcome back 👋".
